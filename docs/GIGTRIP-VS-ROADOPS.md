# GigTrip vs RoadOps — competitor read (2026-11-17)

Sources: roadops.app (homepage only; pricing/features pages 404), the App Store
listing (v1.22.0, 4.8★ from 64 ratings), and two comparison articles by TourPro
(a competitor — treat as their claims). No independent reviews or funding news found.

## What RoadOps is
- A shipped, offline-first tour-management app: iOS, iPad, Android, web, Mac (Apple silicon). No Windows desktop app.
- Founder was lead developer / VP Engineering at Master Tour (2016–2022). Releases almost weekly.
- Sold to tour managers, production managers and coordinators. Reviews are mostly Master Tour switchers.
- Pricing by team size, all features in every plan: free ≤5, $54/mo ≤25, $89/mo ≤50, $149/mo unlimited.

## What it has that GigTrip does not yet
- Real product: accounts, sync, native apps, offline editing.
- AI import: forward confirmations to an email address, paste text, screenshots, venue tech packs.
- Push notifications with read receipts; automatic change alerts inside 24 hours.
- Live flight tracking + flight board; travel-agent integrations (The Optimists, CoTour, Lock Travel).
- PDFs: day sheet, guest list for box office, rooming list. iCal/Google feeds. Home-screen widget.
- Team Connect (headliner + support, festival + artists). Tasks. Developer API / MCP.
- Master Tour migration import.

## Where GigTrip is already ahead (by design)
- The management office: a roster of many artists, season board with pencil/hold/confirmed, offers → holds.
  RoadOps is one touring team at a time.
- Money: deals, night-of settlement, deposits, tour budget, commission, per diems, "my money".
  RoadOps lists no settlement, finance, budget or deal features.
- Advancing: per-show advance checklist from a template with owners and due dates.
  TourPro says RoadOps has "no configurable advance management".
- Roles built for the people: management, tour manager, artist, crew, venue — each with its own home,
  and three money visibility levels. RoadOps has Admin / Member / Custom; nothing for artist, venue or promoter.
- Who brings what, per person and per venue.
- Spreadsheet/ClickUp import with a "where it went" receipt and undo.
- Works on any device with a browser, Windows included.

## What to build to win
1. Custom views — IN THE PROTOTYPE since 2026-11-17 (RoadOps has none beyond colours and toggling sections): saved views and column picker on the
   season board, custom fields on shows/people, a home page each person can arrange, company branding.
2. Table stakes to stop losing on a checklist: offline, forward-an-email / AI import, push with read receipts,
   PDFs (day sheet, guest list, rooming list), live flight status, calendar feeds.
3. Switching tools — IN THE PROTOTYPE since 2026-11-17 (needs a real export from each to verify column names): import from a RoadOps export and from Master Tour, done-for-you migration, run both in
   parallel for a tour.
4. Pricing: per company, unlimited free seats for artists/crew/venues; RoadOps charges on total head-count.
5. Trust for big offices: published privacy policy, SOC 2 path, SSO, audit log. RoadOps states AWS + encryption
   in transit only; its privacy page is empty.
6. Open API/MCP from day one (they have it).

## Positioning
RoadOps = the tour manager's phone on the road. GigTrip = the management company's whole year: dates, deals,
money and every artist, with the road built in. Sell to the office that RoadOps doesn't serve; the TM comes with it.
