# UAT — 2026-11-17

Second full walk, after: PIN + passkeys + recovery email, "Everyone's staying
here", shared check-in first, Find places, and "Put it to a vote".

How it was done: phone size (375px, dark) for every role; desktop (1280px,
light) for layout. A brand-new person and an organizer + guest pair were
walked through the real screens on a throwaway trip dated to be "happening
now" (Los Angeles, Nov 16–19), so Today, Find places and the join flow were
all live. The demo trips were read-only: their screens were read as text, with
no screenshots, so spacing and polish on those roles is judged from structure.
Throwaway data was deleted afterwards.

## Verdict
The core loop now holds together end to end: a stranger can create a trip,
add a stay, find dinner nearby, put it on the calendar and have a guest join
from the link without a dead end, and every role sees only what it should.
What hurts is ordering and timing — finished trips come first on My trips,
Home doesn't yet know what to show *during* a trip, and one search fallback
breaks the "only near my trip" promise.

## Findings (ranked)
**All twelve, and the small wording list, were fixed the same day (2026-11-17):**
My trips order with a "Past trips" section (1); search fallback capped at a day
trip (2); a during-the-trip Home — one "next", no RSVP/arrival/packing prompts,
compact invite, no readiness list (3); plan form defaults to today (4); street
address on the place line (5); recovery email only offered once it can send,
switching on by itself when the secrets are added (6); one optional last
sign-up step instead of two (7); "Username, PIN & passkeys" (8); business
invite wording (9); private attendees see the real head count (10); Find
places hidden after the trip and added to Today (11); Continue stays in reach
on Create (12). Also found and fixed while re-testing: the My trips header
ran off a phone screen, and a closed sheet could be re-submitted.

1. **My trips puts finished trips first.** A returning person with nine
   trips sees five "Trip complete" cards before the trip that's 8 days away
   (it's below the fold on a phone). → Order: happening now, then upcoming
   soonest-first, then finished trips under a "Past trips" heading.
   Affects: everyone with more than a few trips.
2. **Hotel/place search can still leave the city.** On a Los Angeles trip,
   typing "Ace Hotel" in the stay form offered Palm Springs (99 mi),
   Pittsburgh (2,136 mi), Toronto, New York and Japan — the far-away fallback
   has no limit when nothing local matches, and the first suggestion is easy
   to accept by mistake. → Cap the fallback (about a day trip, ~150 mi); past
   that, say "Nothing by that name near Los Angeles". Affects: stay form,
   plan form, Find places search.
3. **Home doesn't have a "during the trip" mode.** On day 2 of a live trip:
   - Today says "Next · in 3 hr — Dinner at Tokyo Haus" while the "Next up"
     card below says "XLanes LA · 3:00 PM", which had already started.
   - A guest is still asked "Are you going?" and told to "Add your flight —
     so we know when you land".
   - The organizer still gets the large invite card and "Collect flights".
   → While Today is showing, drop "Next up", RSVP and arrival to-dos, and
   shrink the invite card (same idea as the after-trip fix).
   Affects: Home, organizer and guest, any trip in progress.
4. **Adding a plan mid-trip defaults to a day that's over.** The form's Day
   starts on the trip's first day (Nov 16 on Nov 17), also when coming from
   Find places. → Default to today while the trip is on. Affects: organizer.
5. **Plans from Find places show the name twice and lose the address.**
   Cards read "XLanes LA / 📍 XLanes LA" and "Dinner at Tokyo Haus / 📍 Tokyo
   Haus". → Save the street address as the place line.
   `img/2026-11-17-plan-name-twice.jpg`. Affects: Calendar, Today, map cards.
6. **Recovery email is offered but can't send.** Sign-up asks for an email
   "so we can send you a reset code"; the sender still isn't connected, so
   "Email me a reset code" answers "Email isn't set up yet". → Owner: add the
   two Brevo secrets; or hide the step until they exist. Affects: trust,
   everyone who forgets a PIN.
7. **Five sheets between "Create trip" and the trip.** Username → PIN → PIN
   again → recovery email → passkey offer. Each is reasonable; together it's
   a long tail for someone who just wanted to see their trip. → Fold email
   and passkey into one optional "easier to get back in" step, or leave the
   passkey to the later prompt that already exists. Affects: new people.
8. **PIN, passkeys and recovery email hide behind "Switch username".** The
   settings sheet's only button in that section is "Switch username", and
   the line above says "Enter it on any phone…" with no mention of the PIN.
   → Label it "Username, PIN & passkeys". Affects: all roles, Settings.
9. **Business invite card says "split costs".** "Anyone with this link can
   join, add their flight and split costs" on a reimbursable-expenses trip.
   → Use the trip-type wording. Affects: business organizers, Home.
10. **Private-event attendee sees "2 Going"** (themself + the organizer)
    under a header that says 36 people. → Show the real count or hide the
    tile on private trips. Affects: private business attendees, Home.
11. **Find places is in the wrong places at the edges.** It shows on the
    Calendar of finished trips, and it's missing from Today's "Nothing
    planned today" card, where it's the obvious next tap. → Hide after the
    trip; add a "Find something to do" button to that card.
12. **Step 1 of Create: "Continue" drops below the fold** once a destination
    is picked (map + nine photos push it down ~185px on a phone). → Keep the
    button pinned to the bottom, or collapse the map.

## Small wording and polish
- Expense line "split 1 ways"; an expense the only member paid reads
  "— not involved".
- Six restaurants in a row all say "right here" (same plaza as the hotel);
  "next door" or a real distance reads better.
- Desktop: the Find places category row scrolls sideways with no hint; the
  Calendar strip wraps to three rows.
- The map's stay card lists names where the Travel card says "Everyone's
  staying here".
- After the trip, Home repeats the balance (wrap card + tile) and still
  shows a "Going" count.

## Inconsistencies across roles/tabs
- "Next" (Today) vs "Next up" (card below) use different rules.
- Day default: stay form uses the trip dates sensibly; plan form doesn't
  account for today.
- "Everyone's staying here" (Travel) vs names (map card).
- Trip-type wording is right in the bar and headings (Agenda, Expenses,
  team) but not in the invite card's sentence.

## Fixed since the last review and still holding
After-trip "That's a wrap" Home (guest, organizer, business); Group pills fit;
"Uncle Mike pays you"; "2 done · 3 left"; "Nudge" + "Add for them"; "Waiting
on" capped; "Add to calendar" for everyone; map pin shows the plan's name;
Venmo to-do hidden on business trips; private attendee sees self + organizer.

## What's already good (keep)
- Create flow: destination suggestions, instant cover photo and map, end
  date following start, the trip named for you.
- Find places end to end: closest-first results, one tap to a pinned plan
  with a sensible title; guests' shortlist → poll lands them on Polls.
- "Everyone's staying here" on by default, with the join trigger — the guest
  was on the stay the moment they joined.
- Invite page: who invited you, tap your name, one field, done; the private
  version asks for a name instead of listing people.
- Role separation and money framing remain the strongest part of the app.

## Not covered

uploads, flight lookup, Venmo hand-off, voting and closing polls (exercised
