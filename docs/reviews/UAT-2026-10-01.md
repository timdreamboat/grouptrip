# UAT — 2026-10-01

Walked as every role on a phone-sized screen (375px), with a desktop
layout check (1280px) and a light-mode check. First pass (new person +
guest on a friends trip) used screenshots; the second pass (all other roles)
had to read the screens as text because the browser pane was hidden, so
visual polish for those roles is judged from structure and wording only.

## Verdict
GroupTripIt is coherent across roles: the same cards, sheets, badges and
floating actions appear on every trip type, wording follows the trip type
(crew / family / team, Calendar / Agenda, Money / Expenses), money is always
framed around *you*, and private business trips hide exactly what they
should. The rough edges are in the margins: time (a finished trip kept
nagging — fixed today), scale (big events produce 38-row "Waiting on" lists
and 60-entry pickers), and a few words that don't fit the trip type.

## Fixed during this review (2026-10-01)
1. Finished trips: Home now leads with a "That's a wrap" card (settle up /
   expense report + photos) and drops RSVP, to-dos, open polls, weather,
   arrivals — guests and organizers.
2. Group pill row (Polls · Photos · Lists · People) fits at 375px.
3. Landing chip shortened to "Free · no account needed · works on any phone"
   and wraps.
Also shipped today: PIN recovery by email ("Forgot PIN? → Email me a reset
code"), prompted once after creating a PIN.

## Findings (ranked)
1. **"Add your Venmo — so friends can pay you back" on business trips.**
   Seen on Dreamforce and the Summit as a to-do. Business expenses are
   reimbursed, not split, and "friends" is the wrong word for a team.
   → Hide the Venmo to-do on business trips (or reword to "so the team can
   pay you back" only where splitting exists). Affects: business guests, Home.
2. **"Uncle pays you" / "Grandma → Tim".** First-name shortening turns
   "Uncle Mike" into "Uncle" in settle-up lines (Thanksgiving, Orlando).
   → Keep both words when the first is a family title (Uncle, Aunt, Grandma,
   Grandpa, Dr…), or show full names in settle-up rows. Affects: family trips,
   Money + Home.
3. **Big events flood the organizer.** Gala Home lists 38 "Waiting on" rows
   with a Nudge each; the Agenda "Showing" picker has ~60 entries (with
   duplicate names indistinguishable). → Cap "Waiting on" at 5 with
   "See all 38" and one "Nudge everyone"; make the Showing picker searchable
   above ~12 people and add a disambiguator for duplicate names. Affects:
   organizers of private/large business trips.
4. **Two names for the calendar export.** Guest Calendar FAB says "Add to my
   calendar"; organizer Calendar has "Sync" + "Add plan". → Use "Add to my
   calendar" (or "Sync calendar") for both. Affects: Calendar, all roles.
5. **Travel "Still missing: Tim — Add" (organizer) vs Home "Waiting on Tim —
   Nudge".** Same situation, two different actions. → Offer both ("Nudge" ·
   "Add for them") or use Nudge in both places. Affects: organizer Travel/Home.
6. **To-do count vs ticks.** "Your to-dos — 3 left" with the first visible
   rows already ticked. → Sort unfinished first, or "2 done · 3 left".
   Affects: guest Home.
7. **Calendar map's unlabeled first pin** before any plan is tapped.
   → Label it with the plan name or start on the hotel. Affects: Calendar.
8. **Settings sheet repeats the RSVP control** that Home already has; fine,
   but after the trip ends (now hidden on Home) it should hide here too.
9. **Private-trip attendee sees "Help cover the group list — 2 things nobody's
   bringing yet."** On a 36-person company summit a shared bring-list is
   odd; it's demo data, but consider hiding Lists on private business trips
   unless the organizer adds items.

## Inconsistencies across roles/tabs
- Calendar export: "Add to my calendar" (guest) vs "Sync" (organizer).
- Someone without a flight: "Nudge" on Home vs "Add" on Travel.
- Home "Where we're staying · All travel" vs tab name "Travel" — fine, but
  "Travel" alone would match the bar.
- Balance shown twice on guest Home with two card styles (to-do row "Settle
  up — You owe $241.39" + stat tile "$241.39 You owe"); after today's change
  the finished-trip wrap card is a third style. Pick one "your balance"
  visual.
- Business trips: Money tab is titled "Expenses" (good) but the bottom bar
  still says the type-specific word correctly; Home stat says "To be
  reimbursed" — consistent. Keep.

## What's already good (keep)
- One floating primary action per tab, named for what it adds.
- Role separation is clean: organizer sees invite/readiness/waiting-on and
  "Got it" on incoming payments; guests see RSVP/to-dos/balance and "Mark
  paid"; private attendees see only themselves + organizer with a plain
  explanation.
- Trip-type wording lands: crew/family/team, Calendar/Agenda, "Add to
  agenda", "Team spend", "What you spent — for reimbursement".
- Money explanations are exactly right ("you owe / you lent" per expense,
  the Venmo trust note, "Pay below, then tap Mark paid").
- The invite page reads instantly: who invited you, who's going, tap your
  name, "No account needed. Your username and PIN…".
- Desktop: sidebar with counts, no horizontal overflow at 1280px; light mode
  tokens switch cleanly.
