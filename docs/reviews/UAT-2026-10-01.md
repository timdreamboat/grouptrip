one pattern for cards, sheets, toasts and badges across every tab, money is
framed around *you*, and nothing was broken. The rough edges are about

open polls, and two header rows don't fit a 375px phone.

## Findings (ranked)
1. **A finished trip still asks "Are you going?" and lists to-dos.**
   Lake Tahoe ended Sep 30; on Oct 1 the guest Home still leads with the RSVP
   segmented control, "Your to-dos — 3 left" (including "Pack your bag") and
   "Open polls". → After the end date, Home should lead with settle-up and
   photos, hide RSVP/to-dos, and show polls as decided/closed.
   Affects: guest + organizer Home on every trip type.
   `img/guest-tahoe-home-after-trip.jpg`
2. **Group pill row doesn't fit a phone.** "People" is cut off at the right
   edge on Polls/Photos/Lists/People. → Let the row scroll horizontally with
   a fade, or drop icon labels to icons-only when tight.
   Affects: all roles, Group tabs. `img/guest-group-pills-clipped.jpg`
3. **Landing-page chip is cut off.** "Free · friends join without an account ·
   works on any ph…" overflows at 375px. → Shorten or wrap.
   Affects: brand-new person. `img/new-landing-phone.jpg`
4. **"Add your flight" is ticked done but still counts toward "3 left".**
   The to-do list shows two checked items and says "3 left"; the count and
   the ticks disagree at a glance (the 3 are the unticked ones further down,
   but the first visible rows are done). → Sort unfinished to-dos first, or
   say "2 done · 3 left".
   Affects: guest Home.
5. **Weather card after the trip** says "Forecasts get reliable about 10 days
   before the trip" on a trip that's over. → Hide weather once the trip ends.
   Affects: Home, all roles.
6. **Calendar map shows a lone pin before anything is selected** with no label
   of what it points at (it's the first plan). → Show the selected plan's
   name on the pin or default to the hotel. `img/guest-calendar.jpg`
7. **Settings sheet repeats the RSVP control** that's already on Home; fine,
   but the Home one disappears for finished trips (finding 1) and the
   settings one should then too.

## Inconsistencies across roles/tabs (seen so far)
- Home says "Where we're staying · All travel" while the tab is called
  "Travel" and its heading says "Where we're staying and who lands when" —
  consistent enough, but "All travel" could be "Travel" to match the bar.
- Home's balance card says "Settle up · You owe $241.39" and the Money tab
  says "YOU OWE $241.39 — Pay below, then tap Mark paid"; same number, two
  different card styles. Pick one visual for "your balance".

## What's already good (keep)
- Every tab has one obvious primary action as a floating button, named for
  what it adds.
- Money is explained in the person's own terms (you owe / you lent per
  expense) and the Venmo trust note is exactly right.
- Boarding-pass flight cards and "Still missing — Tim, no flight yet" read
  instantly.
- Lists' "I'll bring it" / "Undo" is the lightest possible interaction.
- The username → PIN → passkey sheets feel like a modern consumer app.
