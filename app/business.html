What each attendee gets

Not the master plan with 40 rows they have to search. Their own version of the event.

📅

Their own agenda

Sessions for everyone, plus only the breakouts, dinners and meetings they're actually in. It adds to their phone's calendar and stays up to date when you change it.

🏨

Flight and hotel in one place

Type a flight number and the times fill in. The hotel, check-in times, confirmation number and directions are one tap away.

🧾

Expenses without the envelope of receipts

Snap the receipt when it happens. Each person gets a ready-made expense report to print or save as a PDF.

🔔

Changes reach them

Room moved? New dinner time? Update it once and everyone's agenda is already correct. New sessions and a morning reminder on each event day reach people who turned on notifications.

🗳

Quick decisions

Entrée choices, which night for the team dinner, who's bringing what. A poll takes ten seconds, and the organizer can see who hasn't answered.

📍

What's around

Coffee, dinner and things to do near the hotel, closest first, with reviews and directions one tap away.

What you get as the organizer

  • Everyone, at a glance. Who has joined, who hasn't, and when each person lands.
  • Sessions for the right people. Mark a breakout for the eight people in it. Only they see it and only they're notified.
  • Each person sees only their own. On a private event, attendees don't see each other's flights, rooms or expenses. You see everything.
  • All the spend in one report. Company-card items and reimbursable expenses together, by person and by category, with the receipt photos. Export it as a spreadsheet or a PDF.
  • One link to send. Add your attendee list ahead of time; each person joins with their name and lands on their own schedule. Nothing to install, no accounts for you to create.

How it works

Set up the event

Dates, hotel, sessions and who each one is for. About as long as building the spreadsheet you'd otherwise send.

Send one link

In the invite email, the team chat, or a calendar invite. People enter their name and they're in.

Change things once

Everyone's agenda updates. After the event, download the expense report.

Plain answers

  • Does it book travel? No. Book flights and rooms the way you do now. GroupTripIt is where everyone sees the result.
  • Do attendees need an app? No. It opens in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen. They pick a username and a PIN the first time.
  • Who can see what? You choose per event: the whole group sees everything (good for a small team), or each person sees only their own schedule, flight, room and expenses. Details are on the privacy page.
  • Is it ready for a 500-person conference? Not yet. It's in pre-release and best for events up to about 60 people.
  • What does it cost? Nothing while it's in pre-release. Pricing for teams will be worked out with the first pilot events.
