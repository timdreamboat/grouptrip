## What the preview cannot show — required for go-live
These are not bugs in the preview; they are the difference between a preview and a product.
1. **Sign-in and real accounts.** Roles are a demo switch. Live: invitations, sign-in, and
   permissions enforced in the database (a determined person can read anything in the preview).
2. **Shared, saved data.** Everything is in one browser. Live: one database, changes seen by everyone at once.
3. **Venue and promoter as separate roles, per show.** A venue contact should get one show only, by invitation.
4. **Guest list cut-off enforced** (requests close at the set time; management can still add).
5. **Real maps and drive times** (Google key), real flight times and live status, calendar feeds that subscribe.
6. **Alerts** by phone and email, with read receipts; an audit trail of who changed what.
7. **Files stored for real** (riders, contracts, receipts, photos) with virus scanning and size limits.
8. **Billing** per active artist, and company settings for it.
9. **Offline use** on the road.
10. **Switch import verified on real exports** — only our own example files have been through it.
